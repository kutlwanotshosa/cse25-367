Who Are We

SpeedWave Internet is a proudly local telecommunications firm launched in 2024. Our core mission is to bridge digital divides by constructing direct FTTH (Fiber to the Home) and FTTB (Fiber to the Business) infrastructure in Gaborone and neighboring regions, empowering communities with reliable, uninterrupted internet speeds.
